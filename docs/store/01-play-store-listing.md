## English (en-US)

### Short description (≤ 80)

```
Find hidden Bluetooth & Wi-Fi devices in exam halls and log incidents offline.
```

### Full description (≤ 4000)

```
Pahara helps exam invigilators find hidden electronic devices in the exam hall – earbuds, smartwatches, phones, hotspots and spy earpieces – and record incidents with evidence.

Everything works on the phone, offline. No account, no sign-in, no ads.

WIRELESS SCANNER
• Lists nearby Bluetooth devices with an easy distance in metres ("very close", "nearby", "far").
• Recognises earbuds, smartwatches, phones, neck-loop and spy earpieces from their signals and names.
• Android also finds classic Bluetooth devices and phone hotspots.
• Take a room baseline at the start: anything new that appears later raises an alert.
• Mark the invigilators' own devices as trusted so they never alert.

FIND THE DEVICE
• Tracking radar: walk around and watch the closeness gauge rise as you get nearer.
• Walk finder: vibrates when you pass near any chosen device.
• Seat sweep: measures the signal seat by seat and points to the most likely seat.

MORE DETECTORS
• Magnetic scanner – finds the magnets inside earpieces, phones and speakers using the phone's compass sensor.
• RF activity meter – shows how busy the wireless air is around you.
• Hidden camera lens finder – uses the flashlight and camera to spot the glint of a lens.

INCIDENT LOG AND REPORT
• Log an incident in seconds: category, severity, seat on the seat map, notes and a photo.
• At the end of the exam, a PDF report with a timeline and evidence charts, ready to share.
• Past exams are kept on the phone in History.

ONE HALL, MANY INVIGILATORS
• Hall link: invigilators' phones join one hall by scanning a QR code over the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
• Everyone sees the shared device list and alerts. Data travels only inside the hall's local network.

OPTIONAL SENSOR NODES (ESP32)
• Small ESP32 boxes placed at the back rows scan all exam long and report to the host phone.
• Set them up from the app over Bluetooth in about a minute. No coding needed.
• Hall map: place the nodes on the seat map and see the likely area of each suspicious device.

THREE LANGUAGES
English, বাংলা and العربية.

HONEST LIMITS
Pahara finds devices that are switched on and transmitting. A device that is fully off, or in airplane mode with Bluetooth off, cannot be detected by any phone app. Distance and position come from signal strength and are approximate. Always confirm in person before acting.

PRIVACY
Pahara has no servers and no accounts. Exam data stays on your phone unless you share a report yourself.
```
